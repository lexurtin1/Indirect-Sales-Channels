# Network Flow Globe

The interactive globe on screen 1 (the Calastone website). Any country can be clicked to see fund flow activity in two directions: where that country's investors put their money, and who buys the funds domiciled there. The website shows enough to be useful. Anything deeper is offered through the Calastone MCP, which is the point of the screen.

This replaces the globe sections of `docs/fund-flow-index.md`. That doc now covers only the published UK FFI data, which appears here as one layer.

---

## 1. Two kinds of data on the globe

| Badge | Data | File | Covers | Shown as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Published** | Calastone Fund Flow Index (UK) | `data/ffi/extracted/ffi.json` | UK investors into UK-domiciled funds, GBP, from public reports | Real figures with a link to the source report |
| **Illustrative** | Synthetic network dataset | `data/network/illustrative-network.json` | 30 markets, 203 investor-to-domicile corridors, 24 months, USD | Figures that show the kind of data Calastone could display |

Every number on screen carries one of these two badges. The illustrative data is generated by `scripts/generate-illustrative-network.mjs` from a fixed seed, so it is the same every time. It is not real Calastone volume and must never be presented as such. Replacing it with real network data needs legal and client-data sign-off first (section 4).

## 2. Levels of data Calastone could display

Each level adds detail. The levels show how much the network knows, and how each level maps to a channel.

| Level | What it shows | Singapore example | Where it appears |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Network** | Total activity, markets, busiest corridors | "Asia-Pacific investors were net buyers in September" | Website, globe at rest |
| **2. Country** | Buys, sells, net flow and orders for a country, in both directions | Investors in Singapore: buys, sells, net, orders. Singapore-domiciled funds: who buys them | Website, country panel |
| **3. Corridor** | One investor country into one fund domicile, with a 24-month trend | Singapore investors into Luxembourg funds | Website, partner list and arcs |
| **4. Composition** | Asset class, equity region, active or passive, share class currency, distributor type | Singapore into Luxembourg: share via private banks; USD share classes | Website shows asset class only. The rest is a locked teaser |
| **5. Signals** | Momentum, turning points, rank changes, fastest-growing corridors | "Singapore investors' fixed income buying is at a 12-month high" | Public MCP |
| **6. Firm level** | A client's own funds by distributor country, or a distributor's own orders | A Luxembourg fund manager sees its own Singapore inflows by distributor | Private MCP, client only |
| **7. Operations** | Straight-through processing rate, manual order share, settlement timeliness by corridor | Manual order share on Singapore to Luxembourg orders | Private MCP and partner benchmarks, aggregated |

Levels 1 to 3 build trust on the website. Level 4 is the teaser. Levels 5 to 7 are why someone installs the MCP or becomes a client.

## 3. Two lenses for every country

Clicking a country opens its panel with a lens toggle.

**Investors in [country]**: where investors based in that country put their money, by fund domicile. Arcs run from the country to each domicile.

**Funds domiciled in [country]**: which countries' investors buy funds domiciled there. Arcs run from each investor country to the selected country.

Domestic flows (Singapore investors into Singapore funds) appear as a ring on the country, not an arc.

### Worked example: Singapore, September 2026 (illustrative)

| Lens | Buys | Sells | Net | Orders | Partners |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Investors in Singapore | 1,459 | 1,594 | −134 | 62,041 | Luxembourg, Singapore, Ireland, UK, Hong Kong, Cayman, Australia, Taiwan |
| Funds domiciled in Singapore | 640 | 693 | −54 | 26,957 | Singapore, Hong Kong, Taiwan, South Korea, Japan, Australia, New Zealand |

USD millions, from `illustrative-network.json`. The generator is set up so that cross-border domiciles (Luxembourg, Ireland, Cayman, UK) sell worldwide, while local domiciles such as Singapore sell mainly within their own region.

## 4. Access and privacy rules

These rules apply in the prototype and would be required with real data. Showing them in the prototype makes the option credible.

| Rule | Prototype setting | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Minimum firms per cell | Hide any corridor built from fewer than 5 distributors or 5 fund managers | Stops a single client's activity being identifiable in a small corridor |
| Rounding on the website | Display to the nearest USD 10m | Aggregate signal without precise client volumes |
| Delay on the website | One month in arrears | Protects clients' current activity |
| No firm names on public surfaces | Website and public MCP show countries, asset classes and types only | Firm-level data belongs to clients |
| Entitlement | Level 6 data only for the signed-in client's own activity | Client data agreements |
| Sign-off | Legal and client-data review before any real data replaces the illustrative file | Calastone's contracts govern how transaction data may be used |

Suppressed corridors are flagged `"suppressed": true` in the data. On screen they appear in the partner list as "Not shown: fewer than 5 firms", and their arcs are not drawn. The illustrative file has 25 of these, so the rule is visible in the demo.

## 5. Globe layers

| Layer | `globe.gl` feature | What it shows | Encoding |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Countries | `polygonsData` from a 110m country GeoJSON | Clickable countries, tinted by the selected metric | Diverging scale: buying in green, selling in red, neutral `--ink-100`. Hover raises the polygon slightly |
| Financial centres | `pointsData` and `labelsData` | Clickable markers for markets too small to click on a 110m map: Singapore, Hong Kong, Luxembourg, Cayman Islands | Teal dot with a label, always visible |
| Selected country arcs | `arcsData` | The top 8 partners for the chosen lens and month | See below |
| Domestic ring | `ringsData` | Domestic flows for the selected country | Ring colour by direction, size by amount |
| Published FFI layer | Toggle | For the UK, switches to the published FFI view (UK investors by fund investment region, as in `fund-flow-index.md`) | "Published" badge |

### Arc encoding

| Property | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| Direction | Arc runs from the investor country to the fund domicile in both lenses, so money always travels the way it moves |
| Colour | Net buying green (`--status-success`), net selling red (`--status-danger`) |
| Width | `0.3 + 2.2 × sqrt(gross / maxGross)`, where gross is buys plus sells and maxGross is the largest corridor shown that month |
| Animation | Constant speed; dash length slightly longer for larger corridors |
| Count | Top 8 by gross. The rest are listed in the panel, not drawn |

### Map data notes

- In Natural Earth 110m files, France and Norway have `ISO_A2 = -99`. Match countries on `ISO_A2_EH` or `ADM0_A3` instead.
- Singapore, Hong Kong and the Cayman Islands are missing or merged at 110m. Luxembourg is tiny. That is why the financial-centre markers exist.
- Countries with no data are drawn in `--ink-100`, with the tooltip "Not in this illustrative dataset".

## 6. Controls

| Control | Options | Default |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Search | Type a country name | Empty |
| Lens | Investors in / Funds domiciled in | Investors in |
| Metric for country tint | Net flow, gross activity, change on 12-month average, orders | Net flow |
| Asset class | All, Equity, Fixed income, Money market, Mixed assets, Property, Alternatives | All |
| Month | Slider across 24 months, with a play button | Latest month |
| Region jump | Europe, Asia-Pacific, Americas, Middle East and Africa | None |

The asset class filter recolours the countries and redraws the arcs using `net_by_asset_class`.

## 7. Country panel

Top to bottom:

1. **Header**: country name, region, lens toggle and the Illustrative badge (Published for the UK FFI layer).
2. **Key figures for the month**: buys, sells, net, orders, and change against the 12-month average.
3. **Partners**: a table with one row per partner country. It shows buys, sells, net, a 24-month sparkline and a firm band (for example "20 to 49 distributors"). Suppressed partners appear as one line with the reason.
4. **Asset classes**: diverging bars for the month.
5. **Trend**: 24-month net flow line, with the selected month marked.
6. **Composition (level 4)**: asset class visible. Distributor type, share class currency, passive share and equity regions are shown blurred, with a lock.
7. **Go deeper (levels 5 to 7)**: three locked cards specific to the country, such as "Fastest-growing corridor into Singapore funds" or "Manual order share on Singapore orders". Then the calls to action:
   - "Query this data in your AI: install the Calastone MCP"
   - "Calastone client? Request your private MCP"
8. **Example questions** for the MCP, built from the selected country and lens, each with a copy button:
   - "Which fund domiciles gained the most from Singapore investors in the last 3 months?"
   - "Show Singapore investors' fixed income flows by month since 2024."
   - "Which Asia-Pacific markets are buying Singapore-domiciled funds?"

## 8. The MCP hook

The globe gives enough at levels 1 to 3 to be worth exploring. Every locked card, blurred figure and example question leads to the MCP.

| Trigger | What the visitor sees | Event recorded for the analytics screen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Clicks a country | Panel opens | `country_open` (country, lens) |
| Changes lens, metric or month | Globe updates | `globe_filter` |
| Clicks a locked card | "Available in the Calastone MCP" with the install options | `lock_click` (card, country) |
| Copies an example question | "Copied. Paste it into Claude or ChatGPT once the MCP is installed" | `question_copy` (question) |
| Clicks install or request private MCP | Navigates to Chat (`/pages/chat`) with public or private tier | `mcp_cta` (tier, country) |

These events feed the MCP analytics screen: which countries and questions drive installs, and which visitors are existing clients. Download MCP on the Screen 1 funnel opens `/pages/chat?from=globe&tier=public&download=1` (and may pass `market`).

## 9. Data file structure

`data/network/illustrative-network.json`:

| Key | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| `illustrative`, `notice` | Always `true`, and the notice text shown in the footer |
| `months` | `2024-10` to `2026-09` |
| `rules` | Minimum firms, rounding, delay |
| `dimensions` | Asset classes, distributor types, equity regions |
| `countries[]` | `iso`, `name`, `lat`, `lng`, `region`, plus `investor_view` and `domicile_view`, each with a 24-month `series` (buys, sells, net, orders) and partner counts |
| `corridors[]` | `investor_country`, `fund_domicile`, `domestic`, `firms_band`, `suppressed`, a 24-month `series` (including `net_by_asset_class`), and `latest` composition: passive share, STP rate, manual order share, distributor type share, equity net by region, share class currency |

Derived figures the page calculates:

- **Change on 12-month average** = this month's net minus the average net of the previous 12 months.
- **Momentum** = average net of the last 3 months divided by average gross of the last 12 months.
- **Domestic share** = gross of the domestic corridor divided by the country's total gross.
